    If you still owe today’s Asr, the Islamic Foundation timetable allows you to pray it during the period just before sunset.

    How cutoffs are shown

    Hanafi cutoffs use sunrise for Fajr, Asr start for Dhuhr, sunset for Asr, Isha start for Maghrib, and the next dawn for Isha. Pray earlier when you can. Your mosque sets its own Jumu’ah congregation time.

    Dhaka and Mymensingh use the published Dhaka table and Mymensingh table based on the Islamic Foundation Bangladesh timetable. Other districts use estimates adjusted for their location. February 29 uses an estimate from adjacent dates.
